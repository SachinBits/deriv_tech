# Withdrawals

This article describes how customers move funds from their account to a bank account.

## Bank transfer processing time

Bank transfer withdrawals take 1 to 3 business days to arrive after the request is processed. Weekends and public holidays are not business days, so a request made on a Friday may arrive the following week.

## Daily limit

The daily withdrawal limit is $10,000. The limit covers the total of all withdrawal requests made by an account in a single calendar day. Requests above the remaining allowance are declined with a message showing the amount still available.

## Cut-off time

Requests submitted after 14:00 SGT are processed the next business day. Requests submitted before the cut-off are processed on the same business day.

## Before you withdraw

The account must have completed identity verification before any withdrawal can be requested. The destination bank account must be in the account holder's own name. Customers can track the status of each request on the Withdrawals page in their dashboard.
