# KYC Identity Verification

Know Your Customer (KYC) checks confirm that every account belongs to a real, identifiable person. Verification is required before funds can leave the platform.

## Accepted documents

We accept one of the following government-issued photo documents: a passport, a national ID card or a driver's licence. The document must be valid, unexpired and fully visible in the photo, with all four corners in frame.

## Review time

KYC review is completed within 24 hours of submission. Customers receive an email when the review is finished. If a document is blurry or cropped, the reviewer rejects it and the customer is asked to upload a clearer copy, which starts a new review.

## Impact on the account

Accounts cannot withdraw funds until verification is approved. Customers can still sign in, view balances and update their profile while the review is in progress.

## Support guidance

Support agents should point customers to the verification page in account settings and explain which documents are accepted. Agents cannot see or edit the uploaded documents.
